The short version

We help you guide your child — we don't spy on them. Eyezonkid never reads private messages, never records the screen continuously, never logs keystrokes and cannot turn on the camera or microphone. We never sell your data.

  • We collect only what's needed to run the features you switch on — like location, app usage and device status.
  • The app always stays visible on your child's device. It is never hidden or installed in secret.
  • You can see, download or delete your family's data at any time.

Who we are

Eyezonkid (“Eyezonkid”, “we”, “us”) provides a parental control app for Android and iPhone and the parent dashboard at eyezonkid.com. Our registered office is at 417, Manhattan Street, Mahagun Moderne, Sector 78, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201304, India.

This policy applies to the Eyezonkid website, the parent app and dashboard, and the Eyezonkid app installed on a child's device (together, the “Service”). It is published in line with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the rules made under it, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Information we collect

From you (the parent or guardian)

  • Account details — name, email address, mobile number and password.
  • Payment details — plan, billing history and GSTIN if you provide one. Card and UPI details are handled by our payment partner; we don't store full card numbers.
  • Support messages — anything you send us by email, phone or chat.

From your child's device (only for features you turn on)

DataWhy we need it

App list & app usage timeScreen time limits, per-app quotas, schedules and install/uninstall alerts.

From our website

Basic technical data such as browser type, pages visited and approximate region, used to keep the site secure and understand what's useful.

What we never collect

  • The content of your child's private chats, messages, emails or calls.
  • Keystrokes, passwords typed on the child's device, or continuous screen recordings.
  • Camera or microphone recordings — the app cannot switch these on.

How we use information

  • To provide the features you choose and show results in your dashboard.
  • To send alerts you've asked for, such as geofence, SOS and uninstall alerts.
  • To process payments, issue GST invoices and prevent fraud.
  • To answer support requests and improve reliability.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not use your child's data for advertising, and we do not build marketing profiles of children.

Children's data & consent

Eyezonkid is designed to be used by a parent or lawful guardian. By installing Eyezonkid on a child's device, you confirm that you are the child's parent or lawful guardian, that you give consent for the child's data to be processed as described here, and that the device belongs to or is used by that child.

We encourage you to tell your child that Eyezonkid is installed and why. The app always stays visible on the device, and screenshot requests are shown to the child, who can allow or deny them.

Sharing & disclosure

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who host our servers, send notifications and process payments, under contracts that require them to protect it.
  • Authorities when required by law, a court order or to protect someone's safety.
  • A successor business if Eyezonkid is merged or acquired, subject to this policy.

Retention & deletion

We keep account data while your account is active. Location and activity history are kept for a limited period so you can view recent trends, then deleted automatically. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your family's data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law, such as tax invoices.

Delete your account and data

Use this page to ask us to delete your EyezonKid parent account and the data linked to it. You'll confirm it's you with a one-time code sent to the mobile number or e-mail you log in with.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, mobile number, e-mail, photo and app PIN
  • Your children's profiles, paired phones, locations, reports, screenshots and voice messages
  • Logged-in devices and sessions. Your plan ends and AutoPay is stopped, so there are no further charges

What we keep

  • Payment records and invoices, without your contact details, for the period Indian tax and accounting laws require
  • Security and audit logs, and a record of this request, to prevent fraud and prove the deletion was done

Our team reviews each request, usually within 7 days. You'll get an e-mail when it's approved or declined. Deleting can't be undone.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and access is limited to staff who need it to run the Service. We use reasonable security practices as required under Indian law. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your dashboard login private.

Found a security issue? Please report it to security@eyezonkid.com and we'll look into it quickly.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, update or delete your personal data and your child's data, withdraw consent, or nominate someone to act for you. Withdrawing consent may stop some features from working. Email privacy@eyezonkid.com and we'll respond within the time required by law.

Cookies

Our website uses only essential cookies needed to keep you signed in and secure. If we add analytics cookies in future, we'll update this policy first.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes. We'll post the new version here with a new “Last updated” date and, for significant changes, notify you by email or in the app.
