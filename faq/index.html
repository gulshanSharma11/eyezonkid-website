General

What is Eyezonkid?

Eyezonkid is a parental control app for Android and iPhone. It helps you see your child's live location, set screen time limits, give apps daily time quotas and block apps or websites — without reading their private chats.

Does Eyezonkid work on both Android and iPhone?

Yes. Eyezonkid offers the same features on Android and iPhone, so one parent dashboard works for every child's device.

How long does it take to set up parental controls?

A few minutes. Create your account, install Eyezonkid on your child's phone, set your rules, and start seeing activity and alerts on your dashboard.

Is there a free parental control app for Android?

Google Family Link is free and covers basic screen time, app approval and location on Android. Eyezonkid is a paid app that adds per-app time quotas, parent-to-child messages and the same features on iPhone, at ₹999 per year with GST included .

Privacy & safety

Does Eyezonkid read my child's messages?

No. Eyezonkid never accesses, reads or scans private messages. It also never records the screen continuously, never logs keystrokes and cannot turn on the camera or microphone.

Will my child know Eyezonkid is installed?

Yes. Eyezonkid always stays visible on your child's device. It is never hidden, disguised or installed in secret.

How do on-demand screenshots work?

You can request a single screenshot from the dashboard. Your child's device shows the request and your child can allow or deny it. Eyezonkid never records the screen in the background.

Can I control WhatsApp without reading my child's chats?

Yes. Eyezonkid lets you set a daily time limit for WhatsApp or block it on a schedule, such as during study time. It never reads, scans or stores your child's messages.

Where can I read how my family's data is handled?

Our Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it and how to delete it.

Features

Can I track my child's live location?

Yes. Every plan includes live GPS location tracking and geofencing alerts, so you are notified when your child arrives at or leaves places like school or home.

Can I limit YouTube instead of blocking it completely?

Yes. Eyezonkid supports per-app time quotas, so you can allow something like 45 minutes of YouTube a day instead of an all-or-nothing block. You can also block apps instantly or on a schedule, such as during school hours or bedtime.

How do I set parental controls on YouTube?

Turn on Restricted Mode in YouTube's settings to filter mature videos. Then use Eyezonkid to give YouTube a daily time quota, like 45 minutes, or block it during school hours and bedtime.

Can I block websites and adult content?

Yes. Every plan includes website filtering and Safe Search, so you can block specific sites and keep search results family-friendly.

What happens when my child presses the SOS button?

The SOS emergency button (Family and Premium plans) sends you an instant alert with your child's current location, so you can reach them quickly.

Can I send messages to my child's phone?

Yes. On Family and Premium plans you can send text and voice messages from your dashboard straight to your child's device — useful for reminders like "time for homework".

Setup & devices

How do I install Eyezonkid on my child's phone?

Sign up, choose a plan, then download Eyezonkid on your child's Android phone or iPhone and follow the quick setup guide. See the step-by-step setup guide.

Can my child uninstall Eyezonkid?

Not if you turn on password-protected uninstallation in your dashboard settings. The parent password is then required before Eyezonkid can be removed, and install and uninstall alerts notify you of changes.

How can I reinstall Eyezonkid after uninstallation?

Log in to your dashboard, select the child's device and send a fresh install link. Setting it back up takes about two minutes.

How many devices can I add?

Basic covers one child device, Family covers up to 4 child devices and Premium has no device limit.

Why is my child's location not updating?

Check that location is switched on, the phone has internet, and battery saver isn't restricting Eyezonkid. Our troubleshooting guide walks through each fix.

Plans & billing

How much does Eyezonkid cost in India?

Eyezonkid Premium is ₹999 per year, GST included, for one child device. Extra child devices are ₹199 each, up to 10 devices. It starts with a 3-day free trial, and you can cancel anytime.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 3-day free trial — you pay nothing during the trial and can try every feature before your subscription begins.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no contracts or cancellation fees. Cancel from your account before the next billing date and you won't be charged again.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade from your account at any time. The new plan applies from your next billing cycle unless you upgrade immediately.

Is GST included in the price?

No. Prices are shown before GST, which is added at checkout as required by Indian tax law.
