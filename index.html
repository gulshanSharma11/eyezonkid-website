Parent's Guide

Parental controls, explained simply.

What is a parental control app?

A parental control app lets parents manage a child's phone from their own device. With Eyezonkid you can see your child's live location, set daily screen time limits, block or time-limit apps and websites, and get alerts about important changes. It works on both Android and iPhone.

How do I set up parental controls on my child's phone?

  1. Create your account: Sign up and choose the plan that works for your family.
  2. Install on their device: Download Eyezonkid on your child's Android phone or iPhone and follow the quick setup guide.
  3. Set your rules: Configure screen time limits, app blocks and safe zones in minutes.
  4. Start monitoring: View real-time activity and alerts from your parent dashboard.

How do I limit my child's screen time?

Set a daily screen time limit, then add per-app quotas for the apps that matter most, like 45 minutes of YouTube a day. Schedule blocks for school hours and bedtime so the rules apply automatically, without arguments.

Can I monitor my child's phone without spying on them?

Yes. Eyezonkid shows location, screen time and app activity, but never reads private chats, never logs keystrokes, never records the screen nonstop and never runs hidden. Your child can see the app is installed, which keeps monitoring honest and builds trust.
